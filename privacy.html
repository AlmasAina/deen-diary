Privacy Policy and Terms

Deen Say · Last updated 30 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your full name, email address and gender. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase) and stored only as a secure hash; we never see it.
  • Your progress: the records you enter in the app, such as prayers, Quran and tajweed progress, tasks, notes, garden progress and, only if you turn it on, period days.

Why we collect it

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, save your progress and show it on every device where you sign in. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Sensitive information

Gender and period-tracking information are treated as sensitive. Period tracking is optional and off unless you choose it. It is never included in emails or notifications and is never shown to other users.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database protected by row-level security, so each account can read and change only its own records. A copy is also kept in your browser so the app works offline.

Your choices

  • Change your name or password from the Profile tab.
  • Delete your account from the Profile tab. This permanently removes your account and all your progress.

Terms of use

Deen Say is a personal habit tracker. Points, streaks and the garden are motivational tools only; they do not measure anyone's faith, worship or religious standing. Quran translations shown in the app come from published translations and are credited where shown. Please use the app respectfully and keep your password private.
